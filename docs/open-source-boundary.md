# Open-source boundary

## Included

- public contracts and schemas;
- provider-neutral trusted compiler reference;
- deterministic verification rules;
- approval and authorization record patterns;
- audit reconstruction;
- synthetic fixtures and adversarial tests;
- optional provider adapters and examples;
- contributor tooling and documentation.

## Excluded

- DigiTrust hosted services;
- enterprise multi-agent orchestration;
- customer-specific workflows and policy packs;
- commercial connectors and marketplace packaging;
- managed evidence ingestion and retention;
- customer data, configurations, or credentials;
- production identity, authorization, and secret-management infrastructure;
- private analytics, administration, and product roadmap.

The exclusion is functional, not merely a licensing label. Contributions that would couple the core framework to proprietary customer or SaaS behavior should be implemented as external integrations instead.

## Relationship to DigiTrust

DigiTrust may use or extend the framework, but this repository is independently useful and provider-neutral. The public project is not a source-available edition of the commercial platform and does not expose proprietary platform code.
