# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for all participants, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain security and correctness concerns with evidence.
- Protect private information and avoid publishing credentials or customer data.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to preserve project safety and scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, sexualized content, or repeated bad-faith conduct are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the repository maintainers. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary.
